# Glossary

Terms this operation uses in its own particular way. Industry terms are here
only where the pipeline treats them specifically.

## Pipeline vocabulary

**Answer capsule.** The 50 to 75 word paragraph that opens every H2 section and
answers its question on its own, so a search engine or an assistant can lift it
whole. Over the cap, Stage 4 trims. Rule: `R-content-quality-answer-capsules`.

**Human anchors.** Three elements a brief must supply and a humanized draft must
carry in the body: a Real Story, a POV Anchor, and a Contrarian Note. Missing
all three fails Stage 2.5. `brief_preflight.py` blocks Stage 2 when the brief
does not define them.

**AI tells.** The ban list at `checklist/ai-tells.md`, tiered. Tier 0 is
punctuation and blocks absolutely. HIGH tier blocks entry to Stage 3. MED comes
out unless the sentence structurally depends on it, with the exception logged.
LOW comes out when a tighter alternative exists.

**De-listification.** Stage 2.5 step four. At least 40% of H2 sections have to
be narrative prose rather than bullets, bullet fragments become sentences, and
one Pros and Cons section per article is the maximum.

**Cadence.** Sentence and paragraph length variance. The rhythm rewrite wants at
least one sentence under five words, at least one over 25, paragraph length
variance of 30% or better, and no three consecutive paragraphs opening the same
way.

**Loop budget.** Three, shared across Stage 3 rewrites and Stage 7 failures.
Exceeding it sets `manual-review-required` and stops the run.
Rule: `R-process-loop-budget`.

**Micro-fix budget.** Stage 7 may make about two small corrections. Four or more
means the draft needed a real revision pass, so it routes back to Stage 4
instead. Three is the boundary and gets logged.

**Canonical draft.** The one draft file that counts for QA, chosen in order:
`draft-v2-humanized.md`, then `draft-v2.md`, then `draft.md`.

**Production Notes.** The metadata block at the top of drafts that came out of
Google Docs, carrying the same fields YAML frontmatter carries in repo-native
drafts. Both formats parse through `make_paste_kit.parse_notes`.

**Paste kit.** The package the operator pastes into Framer: body plus every SEO
field, generated by `make_paste_kit.py`.

**Baseline ratchet.** `workflow/qa-baseline.txt`. Known legacy findings the
battery tolerates so new work still fails loudly. Entries only ever leave.

**Sentinel.** A file whose presence means a credential is broken and the run
must halt. See [systems.md](systems.md).

**Meta-QA.** Stage 11's check on the pipeline's own documents, added after a
humanization rollout put 101 em dashes into operational files that Stage 7 never
inspected because it only reads blog drafts.

**Stage numbering.** One authoritative map, in `workflow/pipeline.md`. Negative
stages are the optional front end: -4 pre-flight, -3 gap discovery, -2 brief
generation, -1 topic selection. Stage 2.5 is the humanization pass, added
because editorial kept losing voice arguments inside the Stage 3 panel.

## Subject vocabulary

**Fractional ownership.** Direct co-ownership of a specific property, as opposed
to a share in a fund. PSFnetwork measures it per square foot.

**Reg A.** Regulation A, the exemption that lets a company offer securities to
non-accredited investors with an SEC-qualified offering circular. The structure
behind PSFnetwork's offerings.

**Reg D.** The private-placement exemption, generally accredited investors only,
no qualification review. The contrast article for Reg A is gap topic #13.

**Offering circular.** The disclosure document a Reg A issuer files and the SEC
qualifies. Two articles teach readers to read one and to spot red flags in it.

**K-1.** The partnership tax form a pass-through structure sends its investors,
often later than a 1099, which is why K-1 timing is a recurring reader pain
point and its own article.

**REIT.** A pooled, usually listed vehicle. The comparison against fractional
ownership is the highest-value gap in the pool and nobody owns it.

**Self-directed IRA.** The retirement wrapper that can hold alternative assets,
the subject of the IRA and 401(k) rollover articles.

**GEO.** Generative engine optimisation. Writing so an AI assistant can quote
the article: self-contained answer capsules, question-format headings, FAQ
schema, primary sources cited inline.

**Cannibalization.** Two of our own articles competing for one keyword. Checked
at Stage 1 against the repo, before any drafting. A conflict halts the run.

**Hub and spoke.** A pillar article with supporting articles linking back to it.
Stage -1 always selects hubs before the spokes that depend on them.
