# Product canon

What PSFnetwork is, in the terms content is allowed to use. Everything here is
sourced from the repo. Where the repo is thin, this page says so rather than
filling the gap, because a positioning sentence invented in a draft is exactly
how the 2026-08-11 feedback round happened.

## The model

PSFnetwork sells fractional real estate ownership measured per square foot.
A reader buys a stated number of square feet of a specific property rather than
an undivided percentage or a share in a fund. That measurement is the product's
distinctive claim, and it is the reason gap topic #1, square-foot ownership
explained, exists as a definitional article no competitor covers.

The offerings are structured under Regulation A. The company has built
blockchain infrastructure for on-chain ownership management, ahead of any live
secondary market.

## How to describe it

| Say | Do not say |
|-----|------------|
| Reg A, per-square-foot ownership, with blockchain infrastructure already built | that PSFnetwork runs a traditional structure |
| "if and when a live secondary market calls for it" | that a secondary market is coming, or when |
| target return | the banned word for a promised return | <!-- check-rules: allow -->
| co-investors | shareholders |
| fractional ownership | fractional shares |

The prohibition on "traditional" is D-014, and it came from a customer comment
on a delivered article. The conditional phrasing about a secondary market is
part of the same fix: describing infrastructure that exists is a fact, and
promising a market that does not yet exist is a forward-looking statement the
brand does not make.

US only, English only, by operating decision D-006. Reader personas live in

brief for audience. Batch 2 and Batch 3 articles use scenario-style personas,

## Competitive position

Ten competitor files under `competitors/` carry the current read. The short
version from the roadmap's Semrush pass:

- Ark7 is the only aggressive content player, and its channel is programmatic
  city guides, not comparisons.
- Fundrise and Arrived are brand-traffic businesses. Education content carries
  the remainder, and niche topics beat core topics on their blogs.
- Realbricks proved the stat hook works: "90% of millionaires own real estate"
  drives more traffic than the rest of its blog.
- Nobody owns the fractional-versus-REIT comparison, the platform verification
  question, or square-foot ownership as a concept.

That gap list is the topic pool, tracked in [../topics.md](../topics.md).

## Claims discipline

Every numerical, regulatory, comparative, historical, or named-entity claim
needs a row in the article's `evidence.md` before it can appear in a draft, and
a primary source behind that row. Marketing pages are not sources for
regulatory facts. Unsourceable claims are cut or replaced, never softened into
vagueness and shipped. Rule: `R-content-quality-sources-section`.
