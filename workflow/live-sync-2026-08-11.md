# Live-to-Repo Reconciliation: Batch 1 (Step 1 of the single-source-of-truth plan)

Date: 2026-08-11
Trigger: operator asked why repo, Drive and Framer keep diverging, and approved a reconciliation pass as step 1 (step 2, Framer CMS API delivery, is a separate decision; the operator confirmed the site does not use the CMS API today).
Inputs: the 12 live Batch 1 pages fetched today (see `workflow/live-site-audit-2026-08-11.md`), the 12 canonical repo drafts.

## What this pass discovered

The divergence is larger than "links were added in Framer." The live site carries a full editorial revision of Batch 1 that the repo has never seen: rewritten prose with SEO keyword weaving, 5 restructured articles, restored internal links (relative `./slug` hrefs), inline external authority links, and new TL;DR summary blocks. The revision respects the em-dash ban (zero across all 12 pages) but introduced three lowercase-brand violations and three off-policy external links.

The repo's QA'd drafts and the live articles are now two different generations of the same content. Grafting the live links onto the old drafts was considered and rejected: it would create a third variant that matches neither the site nor the QA baseline.

## What was captured

One faithful markdown capture per live article, committed at:

    workflow/live-captures/2026-08-11/[slug].md

Captures live under `workflow/` rather than in the article folders deliberately: they are forensic snapshots of an external system, not pipeline-authored content, and the link-audit tests that guard `blog/**` correctly refuse known-dead URLs that the live pages contain (see below).

Extraction method: server-rendered HTML, block-level elements (headings, paragraphs, lists, quotes) between the TL;DR block and the CTA, links converted to markdown, bold preserved, responsive duplicate renders deduplicated. Fidelity caveats: stat-card and table layouts are flattened to text; template chrome (nav, CTA, related-article cards, footer) is excluded. Three captures carry a `check-rules: allow` pragma on the line that faithfully reproduces the live lowercase-brand violation.

## Decision needed from the operator

Pick one per the batch (a single decision for all 12 is fine):

**Option A (recommended): adopt the live revision as the new canonical.** The revision is editorially stronger where it counts (links, keyword coverage, humanized prose) and clean on punctuation. Path: run a Stage 7 style QA over each capture, fix the three casing violations and three off-policy links on both sides, rebuild each `draft.md` from its capture (frontmatter kept from the current draft, meta descriptions unchanged), mark as v3 in the changelog. The reits article is the exception: its live copy is the old draft, so it needs the revision applied in Framer, not captured from it.

**Option B: reject the live revision.** Re-deliver the repo drafts to Framer and re-apply only the link retrofit through the pipeline. This discards live-only editorial work of unknown provenance but restores full QA lineage.

Either way, two process rules stop the soup from reforming:

1. No direct content edits in Framer. Every change enters through the repo, or it does not exist.
2. Any future audit that finds live text differing from the repo triggers this same capture-and-decide pass before anything else is edited.

## Open question

Who produced the live revision, and from what source text? The pipeline has no record of it. Knowing this matters for Option A: if the revision came from an edited Drive doc, that doc is the true source and should be captured instead of the rendered HTML.
