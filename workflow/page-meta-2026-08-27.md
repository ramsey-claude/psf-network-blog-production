# Static page titles and meta descriptions, 2026-08-27

Audit finding: six of eight non-article pages carried the bare title
"PSFnetwork" and none of the eight had a meta description. The copy
below was written against each page's live hero content, within the
standing rules: titles at 60 characters or fewer, descriptions 150-160
(legal pages shorter), brand spelled PSFnetwork, no em or en dashes,
no pre-qualification CTA language (client decision 2026-08-18),
waitlist as the standing call to action.

Applied in Framer per page: Pages panel > page settings (gear icon) >
Title and Description. If Framer appends the site name automatically,
drop the "| PSFnetwork" suffix from the title before saving.

free properties, Regulation A structure, per square foot ownership,

income" is used instead of promissory earning language.
