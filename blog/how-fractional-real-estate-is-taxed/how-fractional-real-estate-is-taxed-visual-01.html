How fractional real estate is taxed

A K-1 arrives, not a 1099. Plan your filing around that.

Most platforms hold each property in a pass-through LLC. The LLC pays no tax itself. It reports your share of income, depreciation and any gain, and hands you the paperwork.

1
Property sits in an LLC
The entity holds title and collects rent. It is a pass-through, so it owes no tax of its own.
2
LLC files Form 1065
The partnership return is due March 16, 2026 for tax year 2025.
3
You receive a Schedule K-1
Your share of rental income, depreciation, and capital gain at sale. Not a 1099.
4
It may arrive in September
Operators often file a six-month extension on Form 7004, pushing your K-1 to September 15.
