## Focus keyword
red flags fractional real estate offering circular

## ICP
Investor who has an offering circular open, or is about to fund a deal, and wants to know which sections can hurt them. High intent, one step from committing capital.

## Content Angle
Seven concrete red flags: fee layers that stack beyond the headline, related-party transactions between sponsor and LLC, absent or vague backup-servicer language, projections formatted to read like returns, blind-pool raises with unnamed properties, thin or no completed-exit history, and vague conflict-of-interest sections. For each: where it sits in the circular, what good looks like, what walking away looks like.
