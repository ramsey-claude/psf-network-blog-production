Investing $10,000

Three structures, three different ways to lose money.

At $10,000 you can spread across several positions rather than concentrating in one. The useful question is not which structure returns most, it is which risk you are willing to hold.

Each row fails differently. Fractional equity is exposed to the property and the operator. A REIT ETF moves with the stock market and with rates, whatever the buildings do. Debt pays interest until a borrower defaults. Spreading across all three is not diversification if you do not understand which one you are holding.
