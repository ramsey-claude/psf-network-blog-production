REITs vs fractional real estate

Two ways to own property without owning a building.

Both give you real estate exposure without a mortgage or a tenant call. They differ on the two things that decide whether you can live with them: how fast you can get out, and how much you choose.

The trade is liquidity against choice. A REIT lets you leave on any trading day but never lets you pick the building. A fractional position lets you pick the building and asks you to stay for years. Neither is safer; they fail in different ways.
