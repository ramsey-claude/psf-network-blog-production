## Real Story decision

The other humanized articles open with a named investor. This one does not, and the omission is deliberate.

There is no real PSFnetwork investor story available for this topic. Writing one would mean inventing a customer for a financial services company, which reads as a fabricated testimonial and sits close to FINRA and SEC rules on testimonial and endorsement content. The downside is worse than the cadence benefit.

Instead the opening anchors on a concrete, verifiable contrast: the historical $20,000 down payment against today's $20 share. It is specific and human without attributing anything to a person who does not exist.

If investor relations can supply a real anonymized story, it belongs in the Opening block and would strengthen the piece further. Tracked as an open item.

## Key changes

### AI scaffolding removed

Six paragraphs opened with a literal "**Answer capsule:**" label. The label is an instruction to the writer, not content for the reader, and it announces machine assembly more loudly than any phrasing choice in the article. Removed in all six; the paragraphs themselves became the natural section openers.

### "Guaranteed" eliminated <!-- check-rules: allow -->

Three instances, all in legitimate negations such as "returns are not guaranteed". The brand rule bans the word regardless of polarity because it survives being quoted out of context. Rewrites: <!-- check-rules: allow -->

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| returns are not guaranteed | no return is promised | <!-- check-rules: allow -->
| Returns are not guaranteed at any step. | No step carries a promise. | <!-- check-rules: allow -->
| expects a return promise (no investment offers one) | unchanged, already fixed in a prior pass |

### POV anchor added

The article previously described the square-foot model neutrally, as though PSFnetwork had no opinion about its own product. Replaced with an explicit position:

> "Here is why we built it this way anyway. The industry talks in shares, and a share is an abstraction with no natural size. Ask someone what 0.0417 of an LLC interest means and watch the pause. Ask what 50 square feet is and they picture a room."

Followed by an admission of who the model does not serve, which is what makes the claim credible rather than promotional.

### Contrarian note added

The original had a careful paragraph about cross-platform return comparison being difficult. Sharpened into a position the reader can disagree with:

> "Anyone publishing a clean head-to-head ranking is either not reading their own footnotes or is selling something."

This mirrors the contrarian anchor in best-fractional v2 and is defensible: it follows directly from the fee, vintage, and survivorship-bias explanation that precedes it.

### Cadence interventions

- Short sentences added throughout: "None of this is new.", "Four steps, and they are the same almost everywhere.", "Property values fall.", "Tenants default.", "The operator can fail."
- Long multi-clause sentences retained and added for contrast, including the Regulation D explanation and the survivorship-bias paragraph.
- Opening rewritten from two generic paragraphs into a concrete claim plus an honest trade statement.
