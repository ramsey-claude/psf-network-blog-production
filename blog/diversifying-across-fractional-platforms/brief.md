## Focus keyword
diversify across fractional real estate platforms

## ICP
Investor with several properties on one platform who has conflated property diversification with platform diversification. Needs a framework and the honest costs.

## Content Angle
Define platform risk concretely: operator failure, underwriting concentration, fee-schedule drift, servicing quality. Framework: two to three platforms is a reasonable spread for most, chosen for different structures (fund-style vs single-property vs square-foot presentation) rather than different logos. Costs stated honestly: one K-1 per LLC position, multiple onboarding/KYC flows, minimums fragmenting capital. EDGAR check for each platform.

## Human Anchors

### A. Scenario
Eric, a 44-year-old insurance broker in Baltimore, who diversified into four properties and only later noticed all four sat on the same platform. Scenario framing only; no testimonial claims.

### B. POV Anchor
We want a share of your allocation, not all of it; a reader who holds us alongside a competitor is running the strategy we would run ourselves.

### C. Contrarian Note
Property diversification and platform diversification are different risks wearing the same word. Ten properties on one platform is one underwriting philosophy, one fee schedule, one operations team, and one point of failure. The industry talks constantly about the first kind and almost never about the second, because the second sends money to competitors.
