Crowdfunding vs fractional

One is a category. The other is a product inside it.

The terms get used interchangeably, which hides the fork that actually matters: whether you are buying equity in a property or lending money to a project.

Real estate crowdfunding

The broad category. Equity or debt, one property or many.
Covers any structure where a group of investors funds a real estate project online. A debt product pays interest and matures. An equity fund holds a portfolio.

Fractional real estate

A subset. Equity in one specific property, held through an LLC.
You pick the building, you hold a membership interest, you receive rental distributions and a share of any gain at sale. K-1 at tax time.

Ask which one you are buying before you ask about returns. Debt and equity fail in different ways and are taxed on different forms. A platform that offers both will not always make the distinction obvious on the marketing page.
