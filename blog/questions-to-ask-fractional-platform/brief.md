## Focus keyword
questions to ask fractional real estate platform

## ICP
Investor comparing one or two shortlisted platforms, ready to email support before funding. Wants a concrete checklist with what a good answer looks like.

## Content Angle
Ten questions in four themes: filings and structure (which entity, which framework, where on EDGAR), money custody (escrow, transfer agent), economics (all three fee layers, gross vs net yield presentation), and endgame (exit history, backup servicer, redemption terms). For each: why it matters, what a good written answer contains.

## Human Anchors

### A. Scenario
Hannah, a pharmacist in Minneapolis, drafting an email to a platform's support inbox before funding her account. Scenario framing only; no testimonial claims.

### B. POV Anchor
We think the quality of a platform's answers matters as much as the answers themselves; a support inbox that responds in writing, quickly and specifically, is telling you how it will behave when something goes wrong.

### C. Contrarian Note
Most due-diligence lists tell you to ask about returns first. Returns are the one answer a platform cannot honestly give you; the ten questions worth asking are all about structure, and none of them mention yield.
