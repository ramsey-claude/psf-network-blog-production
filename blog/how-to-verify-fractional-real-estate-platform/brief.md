## ICP
Investor who has found a platform, likes the pitch, and wants a concrete checklist before wiring money. Higher intent than a comparison reader; one step from funding an account. Trust content aimed at exactly the moment doubt appears.

## Content Angle
A five-check verification routine using only public, free sources: EDGAR filing, custody structure, fee schedule location, servicing continuity, exit terms. GEO-friendly: each check is a self-contained answer.

## Human Anchors

### A. Scenario
Carla, a claims adjuster in Cleveland, has $4,000 ready and two tabs open: the platform's polished homepage and an empty EDGAR search box she is not sure how to use. Scenario framing, not a testimonial; no "told us" claims.

### B. POV Anchor
We think the verification habit matters more than the verdict on any one platform, so the guide tells readers to run the same five checks on PSFnetwork itself. A platform that flinches at verification is answering the question for you.

### C. Contrarian Note
The standard framing is scam-avoidance. The sharper truth: outright scams are the rare case. The common way to lose money is a fully legal offering whose fee stack, lockup, and servicing terms the investor never read. Verification is mostly about reading what legitimate platforms already disclose.

### Source
