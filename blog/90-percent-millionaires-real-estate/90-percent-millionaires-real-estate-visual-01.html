The 90 percent claim

The statistic is real. The conclusion drawn from it is not.

"Ninety percent of millionaires own real estate" is one of the most repeated lines in property marketing. The number holds up. What it is used to prove does not.

What the claim implies

Real estate is how they became millionaires.
Usually paired with a quote attributed to Andrew Carnegie. That quote cannot be located in his published works and predates rigorous millionaire research.

What the research says

Around 90 percent of US millionaires own a primary residence.
That is home ownership, not investment property, and it is a correlation with wealth rather than a cause of it. Per Federal Reserve data, employment income and business equity are the top two wealth-creation sources.
