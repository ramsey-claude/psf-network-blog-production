## Focus keyword
fractional real estate 401k rollover

## ICP
Employee with an old employer 401(k), curious whether rollover money can hold fractional real estate. Needs the mechanics and the tripwires before calling a custodian.

## Content Angle
The mechanics in order: direct rollover from the old 401(k) to a self-directed IRA with a custodian that accepts alternative assets, then the three tripwires: UBIT via UDFI when the property carries debt, prohibited transactions and disqualified persons, and valuation/liquidity requirements custodians impose. 2025 contribution limits for context. Close with the honest screen: who should simply leave it in index funds.

## Human Anchors

### A. Scenario
Jason, 52, a project manager in Atlanta with an old employer 401(k) he has been meaning to move for two years. Scenario framing only; no testimonial claims.

### B. POV Anchor
We think the self-directed IRA route is right for a minority of investors and wrong for most, and we would rather say that plainly than collect custodial-friction money from readers who should have kept things simple.

### C. Contrarian Note
Rollover marketing sells control: your money, your assets, your choices. Inside an IRA, control is exactly the thing the rules restrict. The prohibited transaction list exists because the IRS assumes you will try to help yourself, and the penalty for guessing wrong can be the entire account tax status.
